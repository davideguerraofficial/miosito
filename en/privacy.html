Newsletter privacy notice

Who handles your data

The data controller is Davide Guerra. If you have a question about the newsletter or your data, write to contatti@davideguerra.com.

What is collected and why

When you subscribe, we collect your email address and record your consent. They are used only to send updates about books, projects and Kickstarter campaigns.

How subscription works

Subscription uses a double confirmation: after completing the form, you receive an email and are added to the list only after choosing to confirm.

Where it is stored

Contacts are managed through Brevo, the service that delivers the newsletter. Email addresses are not saved in the public source code of this website or on GitHub.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email. You may also ask to access, correct or delete your data by writing to the address above.

In short

No third-party advertising. No fixed schedule. Only project updates when there is something real to share.
