Writing, observation and practical work: a personal path made of attempts, notes and things still to learn.

PlaceFoggia, Italy

Davide Guerra was born in Foggia, Italy, on 25 July 2004. As a child, he collected projects, stories and objects that could be imagined differently; his attention often settled on the hidden detail, the part that deserved to be understood before it changed shape.

During his school years, those ideas waited in the background. After graduating, he began working as a maintenance technician and, with afternoons free from studying again, tried to return to them. There were half-finished notes, ideas that had seemed lost and pages almost complete: he follows them slowly, one at a time.

The Art of Solitude became his first published book. Davide is now continuing work on Daniel Belmont, an investigative thriller, alongside small projects that may move beyond the page.

Writing is one part of his language. The other runs through his hands: observing a mechanism, taking it apart with care, understanding what is missing. That is where ideas for design, personal objects and a possible videogame project begin to take shape.

The mark

A sign that tells the path.

It does not describe an arrival point: it brings together three ways of working. The opening pages speak of writing, the circular movement recalls time and care for mechanisms, and the line pointing right suggests a direction to follow patiently.
