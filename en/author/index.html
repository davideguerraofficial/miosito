A few words
about me.

Writing, practical work and small projects, taken forward slowly in my spare time.

DG
Born25 July 2004
PlaceFoggia, Italy

Davide Guerra was born in Foggia, Italy, on 25 July 2004. As a child, he enjoyed making up stories, taking notes and thinking about different ideas, mostly out of curiosity.

During school, those things stayed in the background for a while. After graduating, he began working as a maintenance technician and, in his spare time, returned to writing and organising his notes.

The Art of Solitude is his first published book. He is now working on Daniel Belmont and following a few small projects, without rushing them.

He is also interested in objects and how they work. That is why he keeps room for design ideas or different projects when the time feels right.

The mark

A sign that tells the path.
