About

Davide Guerra

He writes stories, collects fragments and develops projects from the things that deserve a second look.

Davide Guerra was born in Foggia, Italy, on 25 July 2004. He works as a maintenance technician - a job grounded in attention, method and concrete problems to solve.

Writing happens in the space around the working day. His first published book, The Art of Solitude, gathers fragments, thoughts and meditations. His current project, Daniel Belmont - The Day No One Called, brings that same attention to detail into an investigation.

Alongside books, he develops digital ideas and personal projects. They are not presented as promises; they are documented when there is something real to share.
