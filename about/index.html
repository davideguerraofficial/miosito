Davide Guerra

Write.
Imagine. Build.

Author and project creator, interested in the things that can become stories, objects or useful tools.

Davide Guerra was born in Foggia, Italy, on 25 July 2004. He works as a maintenance technician, a job grounded in attention, method and concrete problems to solve.

Writing is a central part of his creative work, but not the only one. The Art of Solitude began as a collection of fragments and meditations; Daniel Belmont turns close observation into an investigation.

Alongside books, he develops digital concepts, tools and personal objects. One is a watch study exploring the relationship between time, form and everyday use. Ideas are shown when they reach a concrete direction.
