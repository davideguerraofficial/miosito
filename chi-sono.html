PlaceFoggia, Italy

Davide Guerra was born in Foggia, Italy, on 25 July 2004. As a child, he collected projects, stories and objects that could be imagined differently; his attention often settled on the hidden point, the part that deserved to be understood before it changed shape.

During his school years, those ideas waited in the background. After graduating, he began working as a maintenance technician and, with afternoons freed from studying, found his way back to them. Half-finished notes, ideas that had seemed lost, pages almost complete: this time, he decided to take them further.

The Art of Solitude, already close to its final form, became his first published book. Davide is now working on Daniel Belmont, an investigative thriller, as well as other projects made to move beyond the page.

Writing is one part of his language. The other runs through his hands: observing a mechanism, taking it apart with care, understanding what is missing. That is where ideas for design, personal objects and a possible videogame project begin to take shape.
